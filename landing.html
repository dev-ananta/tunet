OUR STORY

Bridging gaps, building connections, changing lives.

At TuNet, our mission is to bridge the gap between students who need help and students who can give it. Leah, one of our co-founders, was diagnosed with ADHD and struggled to find a tutor who truly understood her learning needs. Traditional tutoring left her frustrated until a high school tutor mentored her with patience and taught strategies she still uses today.

Jahaan, our other co-founder, faced a different challenge: long hours at a physically demanding minimum-wage job that left little time for school or growth. Freelance tutoring became sustainable and meaningful work, and TuNet was born to empower students through relatable role models while giving high schoolers flexible work that builds confidence, leadership, and purpose.
